Colors

Each color means one thing, in the app and in the lessons. Rail, Aqua and Teal carry the identity.

  • Rail

    #001a1f

    Depth and security. The ground for title cards, motion graphics and the lower third.

  • Aqua

    #4ae6d6

    Live and confirmed. The key-term highlight and the tall brand slab. A fill only, with dark text on it.

  • Teal

    #006060

    Action. Primary buttons, links and the border of a correct answer.

  • Raised

    #e8eff0

    Backdrop behind the avatar.

  • Surface

    #ffffff

    Cards, such as the knowledge check.

  • Bone

    #f1f6f7

    Text on Rail.

  • Muted

    #96b1b4

    Eyebrows and labels on Rail.

  • Ink

    #04191e

    Text on white cards and on Aqua.

  • Amber

    #ffb135

    Watch out, pending.

  • Flare

    #c5002b

    Not covered, incorrect.
